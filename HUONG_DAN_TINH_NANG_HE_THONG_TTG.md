# HUONG DAN TINH NANG HE THONG TTG

Tai lieu nay tom tat cac chuc nang chinh cua he thong TTG theo 02 nhom nguoi dung: nguoi dung thuong va quan tri vien.

## 1. Chuc nang nguoi dung thuong

### 1.1. Dang nhap va dang ky tai khoan

- Nguoi dung co the dang nhap bang email va mat khau da dang ky.
- Nguoi dung moi co the tao tai khoan tai trang dang ky.
- Giao dien dang nhap va dang ky su dung mau do chu dao de dong bo voi nhan dien he thong.
- Sau khi dang nhap, nguoi dung co the truy cap cac chuc nang danh cho tai khoan ca nhan.

### 1.2. Xem danh muc va danh sach san pham

- Nguoi dung co the xem danh sach san pham theo danh muc.
- Nguoi dung co the xem toan bo san pham tai trang danh muc tong hop.
- Nguoi dung co the xem san pham theo don vi cung cap.
- Danh sach san pham ho tro hien thi dang luoi va phan trang.
- Moi the san pham hien thi hinh anh, ten san pham, don vi cung cap, trang thai va mo ta tom tat neu san pham co mo ta.

### 1.3. Tim kiem va loc san pham

- Thanh tim kiem nhanh cho phep loc san pham tren trang theo ten, don vi cung cap hoac ma san pham.
- Bo loc danh muc cho phep chuyen nhanh sang danh muc san pham can xem.
- Bo loc don vi cung cap cho phep hien thi san pham cua mot don vi cu the.
- Bo loc trang thai cho phep loc nhanh cac san pham dang hien thi tren trang.
- Nut lam moi bo loc dua trang ve trang thai mac dinh cua khu vuc dang xem.

### 1.4. Danh sach yeu thich

- Nguoi dung da dang nhap co the bam bieu tuong trai tim tren the san pham de them hoac bo san pham khoi danh sach yeu thich.
- Nut trai tim tren the san pham nam cung hang voi ten san pham va can sat ben phai de thao tac nhanh.
- Nut `Danh sach yeu thich` tren thanh bo loc cho phep chi hien thi cac san pham da duoc danh dau yeu thich.
- Trang thai yeu thich duoc cap nhat truc tiep tren giao dien sau khi nguoi dung thao tac.

### 1.5. Xem chi tiet san pham

- Nguoi dung co the mo trang chi tiet de xem thong tin day du cua san pham.
- Trang chi tiet san pham hien thi hinh anh, ten san pham, thong tin mo ta va cac thong tin lien quan duoc cau hinh trong he thong.
- Neu san pham khong co mo ta ngan, khu vuc mo ta tren the san pham de trong thay vi hien thi noi dung thay the.

### 1.6. Quan ly tai khoan ca nhan

- Nguoi dung da dang nhap co the xem va cap nhat thong tin tai khoan.
- Nguoi dung co the doi mat khau theo chinh sach bao mat cua he thong.
- Nguoi dung co the dang xuat khoi tai khoan khi khong con su dung.

### 1.7. Tin tuc, noi dung va tai lieu cong khai

- Nguoi dung co the xem cac trang noi dung, tin tuc hoac khu vuc thong tin duoc quan tri vien cong bo.
- Cac tai lieu cong khai trong kho tai lieu co the duoc truy cap theo phan quyen hien hanh.
- Noi dung hien thi phu thuoc vao trang thai xuat ban va cau hinh cua quan tri vien.

## 2. Chuc nang quan tri vien

### 2.1. Dang nhap khu vuc quan tri

- Quan tri vien dang nhap bang tai khoan co quyen truy cap trang quan tri.
- He thong hien thi menu va chuc nang theo quyen cua tung tai khoan quan tri.
- Cac thao tac quan tri quan trong duoc bao ve bang phan quyen va chong gia mao yeu cau.

### 2.2. Quan ly san pham

- Quan tri vien co the tao moi, sua, xoa va tim kiem san pham.
- Quan tri vien co the cau hinh ten san pham, ma san pham, mo ta, hinh anh, danh muc, don vi cung cap va trang thai hien thi.
- Quan tri vien co the quan ly thuoc tinh, thong so ky thuat, the san pham va cac thong tin SEO cua san pham.
- Quan tri vien co the xuat ban hoac an san pham tuy theo nhu cau van hanh.

### 2.3. Quan ly danh muc

- Quan tri vien co the tao moi va sap xep danh muc san pham.
- Quan tri vien co the gan san pham vao danh muc phu hop.
- Quan tri vien co the cau hinh ten, mo ta, hinh anh, SEO va trang thai xuat ban cua danh muc.
- Danh muc duoc su dung tren giao dien nguoi dung de dieu huong va loc san pham.

### 2.4. Quan ly don vi cung cap

- Quan tri vien co the tao moi, sua va an hien don vi cung cap.
- Quan tri vien co the gan san pham voi don vi cung cap tuong ung.
- Thong tin don vi cung cap duoc dung trong bo loc va tren the san pham ngoai giao dien nguoi dung.

### 2.5. Quan ly khach hang va tai khoan

- Quan tri vien co the xem danh sach tai khoan nguoi dung.
- Quan tri vien co the tim kiem nguoi dung theo thong tin tai khoan.
- Quan tri vien co the cap nhat thong tin tai khoan, vai tro va trang thai hoat dong theo quyen duoc cap.
- Quan tri vien co the quan ly vai tro de gioi han cac khu vuc chuc nang trong he thong.

### 2.6. Quan ly kho tai lieu

- Quan tri vien co quyen phu hop co the truy cap plugin kho tai lieu.
- Quan tri vien co the tao, sua, xoa va xuat ban tai lieu.
- He thong tu dong ho tro sinh slug duy nhat cho tai lieu khi can thiet.
- Menu cau hinh kho tai lieu hien thi theo dung quyen quan tri duoc cap.

### 2.7. Bao cao Logistics

- Quan tri vien co the truy cap khu vuc bao cao Logistics theo quyen duoc cap.
- He thong ho tro quan ly va theo doi cac chi so bao cao Logistics.
- Cac chi so bao cao duoc xu ly an toan khi du lieu cau hinh thay doi.
- Form ly do tra ve trong bao cao Logistics da duoc lien ket dung voi xu ly tren server.

### 2.8. Quan ly slider Swiper

- Quan tri vien co the cau hinh slider hien thi tren giao dien nguoi dung.
- Quan tri vien co the them, sua, xoa slide va cau hinh noi dung hien thi cua slide.
- He thong doc dung cau hinh phan trang slider theo tung store.
- Du lieu slide rong hoac khong hop le duoc xu ly an toan de tranh loi hien thi.

### 2.9. Quan ly noi dung he thong

- Quan tri vien co the quan ly cac trang noi dung, tin tuc va thanh phan hien thi cong khai neu duoc phan quyen.
- Quan tri vien co the cau hinh SEO, tieu de, noi dung va trang thai xuat ban cua cac trang noi dung.
- Cac noi dung sau khi xuat ban se duoc hien thi o khu vuc tuong ung tren giao dien nguoi dung.

### 2.10. Cau hinh he thong

- Quan tri vien co quyen co the cau hinh thong tin store, ngon ngu, giao dien, media, email va cac thiet lap van hanh khac.
- Trang dashboard va cac khu vuc cau hinh chi hien thi nhung thong tin phu hop voi he thong hien tai.
- Cac link va form quan tri da duoc dieu chinh de tranh tro toi khu vuc khong con su dung.

### 2.11. Phan quyen va an toan

- He thong su dung vai tro va permission de kiem soat quyen truy cap.
- Quan tri vien chi nhin thay cac menu va chuc nang phu hop voi quyen cua minh.
- Cac thao tac them moi hoac cap nhat du lieu quan trong duoc kiem tra quyen truoc khi xu ly.
- Cac endpoint quan tri quan trong duoc bao ve bang antiforgery token khi phu hop.

### 2.12. Kiem tra va van hanh

- Sau khi cap nhat code, project `Nop.Web` da duoc build thanh cong bang lenh build ra thu muc tam.
- Lenh chay ung dung goi y: `dotnet run --project "D:\thayhai\banhang\src\Presentation\Nop.Web\Nop.Web.csproj"`.
- Neu file runtime dang bi khoa boi process dang chay, co the build kiem tra bang tham so `-o` de xuat ra thu muc tam.
